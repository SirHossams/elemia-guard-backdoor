We are responsible for developing and updating the simulation program of the system in which we calculate the metrics, status and loggings of the network, in addition to try and simulate realistic attacks and giving suggested patches to prevent it and trying some solutions and strategies to mitigate the effects of the cyber attacks. All that by seeing the bird-eye layout of the network (seeing everything from above) and in that case we try to and learn taking decisions. We test it with the networking services like switching, routing, DHCP, DNS, NAT, etc.

Moreover, the mission of the team is to build the controller program that controls the system and monitoring it and its status by connecting it to one of the devices that contains one of the services of the ELEMIA GUARD system inside the network enterprise (or the simulated network enterprise).
